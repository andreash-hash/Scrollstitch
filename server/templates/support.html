How do I use it?

  • Record your screen while scrolling steadily through the content you want to keep.
  • Open ScrollStitch and pick that recording from your photo library.
  • The app finds where the frames overlap and joins them into one tall image.
  • Save it to Photos or export it as a PDF.

Scroll at a steady pace

The app matches frames by finding where they overlap. A smooth, unhurried scroll gives it plenty to work with. Very fast flicks can jump past a whole screen of content, leaving nothing to match on.

My result has a gap marked in it

That means two frames had no measurable overlap — usually a fast scroll or a jump. Rather than silently inventing content or dropping it, the app joins them and tells you exactly where the uncertainty is. Re-record that stretch more slowly and it will stitch cleanly.

Why does it take a moment?

Every frame is compared against the last one to find the true overlap, which is what keeps the seams invisible. Longer recordings mean more comparisons. You can leave the app while it works — if you allow notifications, it will tell you when the result is ready.

Managing your subscription

  • Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.
  • Cancel any time in the Settings app, under your Apple Account, at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • Changed device, or reinstalled? Use Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

Deleting your data

Frames are uploaded to our server only to be stitched, and are deleted as soon as the job finishes. Nothing is kept and no account is required. See the privacy policy for the full detail.
